# Food handling checks
Checked 2026-11-10 against linked US government guidance. Apply appropriate local guidance elsewhere and recheck for the actual menu.
- FoodSafety.gov: refrigerate/freeze perishables within 2 hours; 1 hour if exposed above 90 F. Refrigerator 40 F or below, freezer 0 F or below.
- USDA: hot holding 140 F or warmer, cold holding 40 F or below. Divide large quantities into shallow containers for rapid cooling; don't leave food out to cool overnight.
- Cold storage chart: many cooked leftovers keep 3–4 days refrigerated; use the actual food entry, handling conditions and package guidance, not a universal expiry rule.
- Use food thermometers and food-specific safe internal temperatures. Don't infer safety from color or a task marked done.
- FARE: tiny transferred allergen proteins can cause reactions. Separate utensils/handling matter; removing a visible ingredient is not verification.

Record cook/supplier verifier, handling plan, source/date and unresolved questions. An automated recipe filter cannot certify labels, cross-contact or a real kitchen. Never depend solely on a reminder for temperature/time control. Do not label unknown handling safe.
